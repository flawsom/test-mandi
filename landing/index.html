03 / EMPIRICAL FINDINGS

Regression Discontinuity Results

Does the IMD declaring a region as "rain-deficient" drive agricultural market behavior? We test discontinuity at the official -20% deficit cutoff.

Key Finding: Onions show statistically significant price jumps (p < 0.05) at the threshold, suggesting active hoarding behavior. Other crops show continuous responses.
